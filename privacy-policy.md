<h2>5. Advertising, Consent &amp; Your Choices</h2>
<p>Pointly serves <strong>non-personalised (contextual) advertising only</strong>.</p>
<p>I have deliberately chosen not to use personalised advertising, and I have not integrated a consent management platform (CMP) because I do not need one. Google's requirement for a Google-certified CMP applies to publishers serving <em>personalised</em> ads to users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland; because Pointly requests contextual ads only, that requirement does not apply to this App.</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>No personalisation:</strong> ads are selected from the content of the screen and the general region of the request, not from a profile of you. Google's publisher first-party ID — which exists specifically to personalise ads across a publisher's own apps — is disabled in the App.</li>
  <li><strong>No consent prompt:</strong> there is no consent banner because there is no personalised advertising to consent to.</li>
  <li><strong>Opting out:</strong> you can limit or reset advertising identifiers at any time via your Android device settings (Settings &gt; Google &gt; Ads &gt; Reset or delete advertising ID), or by using the system's "Delete advertising ID" app permission. Doing so affects ad personalisation across all apps on your device.</li>
  <li><strong>Turning ads off in-app:</strong> Pointly will add an in-app option to remove all advertising. Where that option exists, it takes effect immediately and the App stores no record of the choice on any server I control.</li>
</ul>
