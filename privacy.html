Privacy Policy for Pointly

Last updated: 24 September 2026

1. Introduction & Data Controller

Welcome to Pointly ("the App").

Pointly is developed and operated by Scott D ("I", "me", or "my"), an individual based in the United Kingdom.

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018, I act as the Data Controller for personal data processed through the App.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, you can contact me at:

  • Email: privacy@scottdavis.uk
  • Location: United Kingdom

2. Information We Collect

Because Pointly does not require you to register an account, I do not directly collect or store personal details like your name, physical address, or phone number. However, the App relies on third-party services that collect data automatically.

A. Data Collected Automatically (via Third-Party SDKs)

When you use the App, Google services (AdMob and Google Analytics) automatically collect certain technical data, which may include:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID/AAID), unique device identifiers (Android ID).
  • Technical & Device Information: Device model, operating system version, mobile network provider, language settings, and screen resolution.
  • Usage & Interaction Data: Screens visited within the App, session duration, button taps, crash logs, and general performance diagnostics.
  • Location Data: Coarse location (e.g., country or city level derived from IP address). We do not collect precise GPS location.

B. Information You Provide Directly

  • If you contact me for customer support, I will collect your email address and any details you provide in your message in order to resolve your inquiry.

3. How We Use Your Data & Our Legal Bases (UK GDPR)

Under the UK GDPR, data must be processed lawfully. The purposes and legal grounds for processing your data include:

Purpose Type of Data Legal Basis (UK GDPR)
Serving Advertisements (AdMob) Device IDs, Ad interaction data, IP address Consent (for personalised ads & storing/accessing identifiers on your device) / Legitimate Interests (for non-personalised ad delivery & fraud detection).
Analytics & App Improvement (Google Analytics) In-app actions, crash logs, performance metrics Consent (where required by PECR) or Legitimate Interests (to fix bugs and optimise performance).
Customer Support Email address and message contents Legitimate Interests (responding to queries and providing assistance).

4. Third-Party Service Providers

The App integrates tools from Google LLC. These providers process information under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising)
    • Used to serve advertisements within the App. AdMob may use device identifiers and cookies/mobile tracking to show ads relevant to your interests (if consent is given) or contextual ads.
    • Google AdMob Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics for Firebase / Google Analytics (Analytics)
    • Used to understand how users interact with the App, identify crashes, and improve functionality.
    • Google Privacy Policy

5. User Consent & Your Advertising Choices

In accordance with UK and European regulations (UK GDPR and PECR):

  • Consent Banner: If you are located in the UK or EEA, the App will prompt you via a Google-certified Consent Management Platform (CMP) to choose whether you consent to personalised advertising and analytics tracking.
  • Withdrawing Consent: You can change your advertising preferences at any time through the App settings (if implemented) or via your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Reset or Delete advertising ID).

6. International Data Transfers

Because our third-party provider (Google LLC) is based globally, your data may be transferred to and stored on servers located outside the United Kingdom (such as the United States).

These transfers are governed by UK-approved standard safeguards, including the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), ensuring your information remains protected to standards equivalent to the UK GDPR.

7. Data Retention

  • I do not maintain a private server containing your personal data.
  • Data collected via Google AdMob and Google Analytics is retained according to Google's data retention schedules (typically ranging from 2 to 14 months for analytics events).
  • Support emails are deleted once your query has been resolved, unless retention is required for legal reasons.

8. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

If you are located in the UK, you have rights regarding your personal data, including:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of the personal data held about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to Restrict or Object: Object to or restrict certain processing activities.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please email privacy@scottdavis.uk. Note that because data is tied to pseudonymous device identifiers rather than personal profiles, I may request your Google Advertising ID to locate the relevant data.

Right to Complain to the Regulator

You have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK supervisory authority:

  • Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)
  • Website: https://ico.org.uk
  • Helpline: 0303 123 1113

9. Children's Privacy

Pointly is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the UK/EEA where applicable). I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact me immediately, and I will take steps to remove that information.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect updates to the App or changes in law. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. You are advised to review this policy periodically.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, feel free to contact:
