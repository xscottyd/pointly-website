Last updated: 13 November 2026

01 Introduction & Data Controller

Welcome to Pointly ("the App").

Pointly is developed and operated by Scott D ("I", "me", or "my"), an individual based in the United Kingdom.

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018, I act as the Data Controller for personal data processed through the App.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, you can contact me at:

  • Email: privacy@scottdavis.uk
  • Location: United Kingdom

02 Information We Collect

Because Pointly does not require you to register an account, I do not directly collect or store personal details like your name, physical address, or phone number. However, the App includes third-party SDKs that collect data automatically.

A. AdMob (advertising)

When you use the App, Google AdMob automatically collects certain technical data, which may include:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID/AAID), unique device identifiers (Android ID).
  • Technical & Device Information: Device model, operating system version, mobile network provider, language settings, and screen resolution.
  • Ad Interaction Data: Ad impressions, whether an ad was viewed or clicked, and general performance diagnostics used to deliver and improve advertising.
  • Location Data: Coarse location (e.g., country or city level derived from IP address). We do not collect precise GPS location.

B. Google Analytics for Firebase & Firebase Crashlytics

The release build of the App also includes Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, which collect:

  • App activity: app launches and screen names — fixed internal names such as remote or settings, never anything you have typed.
  • Usage events: a small fixed set of signals — welcome screen dismissed, debug overlay toggled, and the kind of Home Assistant endpoint in use (local, Nabu Casa, demo, custom, or unknown).
  • Crash and non-fatal reports: stack trace, device model, Android version, app version, and a short fixed label describing where the failure happened.

No Home Assistant URL, access token, entity ID, room or area name, or target name is ever sent to these services. Analytics and crash collection are disabled in debug builds.

C. Information You Provide Directly

  • If you contact me for customer support, I will collect your email address and any details you provide in your message in order to resolve your inquiry.

03 How We Use Your Data & Our Legal Bases (UK GDPR)

Under the UK GDPR, data must be processed lawfully. The purposes and legal grounds for processing your data include:

Purpose Type of Data Legal Basis (UK GDPR)
Serving Advertisements (AdMob) Device IDs, Ad interaction data, IP address Legitimate Interests (delivering contextual, non-personalised advertising and fraud detection). No consent-based processing is used, because no personalised advertising is served.
Product analytics & crash reporting (Firebase) App instance and advertising IDs, screen and event names, crash stack traces, device and app version Legitimate Interests (finding and fixing crashes, understanding which parts of the App are used). No Home Assistant endpoint, token, or entity data is involved.

04 Third-Party Service Providers

The App integrates tools from Google LLC. These providers process information under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising)
    • Used to serve non-personalised (contextual) advertisements within the App. AdMob may use device identifiers to select and measure ads; no personalised or interest-based advertising is served.
    • Google AdMob Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase (Analytics & Crashlytics)
    • Google Analytics for Firebase records the App's screen names and a fixed vocabulary of usage events. Firebase Crashlytics records crash and non-fatal reports stamped with the build number. Neither receives your Home Assistant configuration or anything you have typed.
    • Firebase Privacy Policy

The App contacts only Google's advertising and analytics services and the Home Assistant instance you configure. It does not contact any other server operated by me.

05 Advertising, Consent & Your Choices

Pointly serves non-personalised (contextual) advertising only.

I have deliberately chosen not to use personalised advertising, and I have not integrated a consent management platform (CMP) because I do not need one. Google's requirement for a Google-certified CMP applies to publishers serving personalised ads to users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland; because Pointly requests contextual ads only, that requirement does not apply to this App.

  • No personalisation: ads are selected from the content of the screen and the general region of the request, not from a profile of you. Google's publisher first-party ID — which exists specifically to personalise ads across a publisher's own apps — is disabled in the App.
  • No consent prompt: there is no consent banner because there is no personalised advertising to consent to.
  • Opting out: you can limit or reset advertising identifiers at any time via your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Reset or delete advertising ID), or by using the system's "Delete advertising ID" app permission. Doing so affects ad personalisation across all apps on your device.
  • Removing ads: the App does not currently offer an in-app purchase to remove advertising.

06 International Data Transfers

Because our third-party providers (Google LLC) are based globally, your data may be transferred to and stored on servers located outside the United Kingdom (such as the United States).

These transfers are governed by UK-approved standard safeguards, including the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), ensuring your information remains protected to standards equivalent to the UK GDPR.

07 Data Retention

  • I do store any data from app users, and nothing is ever sent to Pointly servers
  • Data collected via Google AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase, and Firebase Crashlytics is retained according to Google's data retention schedules (analytics events typically 2 to 14 months; crash reports around 90 days).
  • Support emails are deleted once your query has been resolved, unless retention is required for legal reasons.

08 Your Rights Under UK GDPR

If you are located in the UK, you have rights regarding your personal data, including:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of the personal data held about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to Restrict or Object: Object to or restrict certain processing activities.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please email privacy@scottdavis.uk. Note that because data is tied to pseudonymous device identifiers rather than personal profiles, I may request your Google Advertising ID to locate the relevant data.
